Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 26, 2026  ·  Last updated: May 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Peg Solitaire ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our mobile game application ("App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy.

We do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information. Your gameplay data stays on your device.

Information We Do Not Collect

Peg Solitaire does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or any personal identifiers
  • Location data
  • Contact information or address book data
  • Camera, microphone, or photo library access
  • Login credentials or account information

Local Data Storage

The App stores your game progress (completed levels, star ratings, and settings) locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Advertising

Peg Solitaire uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect certain information to serve relevant ads, including:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID)
  • IP address and general location
  • App usage and interaction data

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with their privacy policy. You can review Google's privacy practices at policies.google.com/privacy.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device's ad settings: on Android, go to Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Third-Party Services

The App integrates Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service operated by Google LLC. We do not control the data practices of this service. Please refer to Google's privacy policy for details.

Children's Privacy

Peg Solitaire is a casual puzzle game suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The App does not require account registration or any personal information to play.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
